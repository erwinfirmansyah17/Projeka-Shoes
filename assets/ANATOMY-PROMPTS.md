# Prompt visual anatomi

Metode: built-in `image_gen`, edit dengan referensi PNG produk yang sudah tersedia. `transparent_background: true` untuk seluruh output. Sumber dipertahankan; gambar baru disimpan dengan akhiran `-anatomy`. WebP dihasilkan melalui encoding Sharp.

## Aero Shift

Input: `assets/source/aero-shift.png`. Output: `assets/source/aero-shift-anatomy.png`, `public/images/aero-shift-anatomy.webp`.

Edit target: the attached fictional sneaker product. Create a premium photorealistic exploded-view anatomy illustration of this exact white and ice-blue sneaker, preserving its upper silhouette, colors, unbranded panels and left-pointing three-quarter camera view. Show exactly three physically plausible complete components separated vertically: complete white/ice-blue mesh upper with laces at top, sculptural white foam midsole in the middle, thin ice-blue and dark rubber outsole at bottom. Align all components in the same perspective, toe left and heel right. Each layer must be whole, not slices of the original photograph. Generous vertical air gaps and padding on all sides. Upper occupies upper half; foam and rubber layers beneath. High-end studio product photography, genuine transparent background, no floor, no text, no arrows, no labels, no logos. This is an illustrative concept anatomy, not engineering documentation.
